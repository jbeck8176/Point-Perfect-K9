About Us

At Point Perfect K9 Academy, we are dedicated to fostering the innate abilities of your hunting partners. With a deep passion for German Shorthaired Pointers and upland environments, we focus on clear, non-verbal communication and natural instincts.

Most issues seen in and out of the home are simple misunderstandings between dog and owner. Our goal is to bridge that gap, giving you the tools to succeed both in the living room and in the field.

Our Training Programs

We approach dog training by building a solid foundation first, then exposing and refining your dog's natural ability in the field.

Obedience Training

Focused on building the initial foundation of creating enjoyable dogs. With this foundation, you can navigate daily life in and out of the house maintaining control of your dog.

The Four Foundational "Walls":

  • Come when I call you (here)
  • Stay where I put you (sit / kennel)
  • Follow me (heel)
  • Walk on a leash
Pricing & Details:
  • $80 per session (30-60 minutes)
  • $50 per additional dog
  • First 15 miles travel free ($10 per 15 miles after)

Upland Bird Work

Building a dog who you want to enjoy the field with requires a calculated introduction to things the pup will experience in the field.

  • Bird Introduction: A safe, calculated introduction to harvested and live birds to build confidence.
  • Gun Introduction: Properly introducing gunfire to avoid gun-shy issues.
  • Hunt Steadiness: Refining your pointing dog to maintain point until you get into range.
Pricing & Details:
  • $45 per lesson (Bird, Gun, or Steadiness)
  • Birds range from $6 to $30 each (most sessions require 5+)
  • Additional travel fees may apply
