About Us

At Point Perfect K9 Academy, we know your dogs aren't just pets—they are cherished members of your family.

Whether we are working with a driven hunting partner or a beloved household companion, our dedication lies in fostering their natural abilities and building a seamless bond between you and your dog.

We uniquely blend the lines of canine education, bringing the proven techniques, clear non-verbal communication, and instinct-driven methods we use with our hunting dogs directly into our everyday obedience training. Most issues seen in and out of the home stem from simple misunderstandings between dog and owner. Our goal is to bridge that gap, equipping you with the tools and confidence to succeed just as much in the living room as you do out in the field.

Our Training Programs

We approach dog training by building a solid foundation first, then exposing and refining your dog's natural ability in the field.

Obedience Training

Our obedience program is geared towards the industry-standard AKC Canine Good Citizen program, focused on building the essential foundation for an enjoyable, well-mannered dog. With this foundation, you can confidently navigate daily life in and out of the house. However, lasting obedience doesn't come from us training your dog—it comes from us training you. Because our program is highly owner-focused, we prioritize teaching you the exact techniques you need to train your own dog, ensuring you always maintain control and confidence in any environment.

The Four Foundational "Walls":

  • Come when I call you (here)
  • Stay where I put you (sit / kennel)
  • Follow me (heel)
  • Walk on a leash
Pricing & Details:
  • $80 per session (30-60 minutes)
  • $50 per additional dog
  • First 15 miles travel free ($10 per 15 miles after)

Upland Bird Work

Building a dog who you want to enjoy the field with requires a calculated introduction to things the pup will experience in the field.

  • Bird Introduction: A safe, calculated introduction to harvested and live birds to build confidence.
  • Gun Introduction: Properly introducing gunfire to avoid gun-shy issues.
  • Hunting Basics: Reinforcing the natural hunting instincts and behaviors in the field.
  • Hunt Steadiness: Refining your pointing dog to establish and maintain point until you get into range and beyond.
  • Hunt Test Preparation: A helping hand in finishing your dog and prepairing for hunt tests or just a season in the field.
Pricing & Details:
  • $45 per lesson (Bird, Gun, or Steadiness)
  • Birds range from $5 to $30 each (most sessions require 3+)
  • Additional travel fees may apply

Breeding Program

Our breeding program goal is simple: Produce true family companion hunting dogs.

Our focus is on producing high-quality German Shorthaired Pointers that excel in the upland field while remaining affectionate companions at home. We prioritize health, temperament, intelligence, and a strong natural drive. These aren't dogs that can live in our homes — they do! They're family members first, hunting partners second, and they're expected to excel at both.
